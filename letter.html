Dear Mummy, ❤️

💌

I may not say it every day, but I hope you know how much you mean to me.

Thank you for every sleepless night, every correction, every sacrifice and every prayer.

Thank you for standing by me, believing in me and loving me even when I don't always get things right.

I pray that God blesses you with long life, good health, peace of mind and endless joy. May He reward you for every good thing you have done.

I hope I grow into someone who makes you proud. I want you to look at me someday and smile, knowing that your sacrifices were never in vain.

No matter how old I become or where life takes me, I'll always be your child.

I love you, Mummy. More than words can say. ❤️

Forever your child,
Rhema ❤️
